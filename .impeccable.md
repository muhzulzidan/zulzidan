## Design Context

### Users
The primary homepage audience is English-speaking owners and decision-makers at
service businesses—especially contractors, logistics companies, local services,
and agencies—who are evaluating whether Zulzidan is a credible website partner.
Their main jobs are to understand the offer quickly, review relevant work, assess
delivery risk, and request a useful first conversation or website audit.

Indonesian businesses remain an important secondary audience and are served by
dedicated Bahasa Indonesia landing pages with IDR pricing, local SEO, and
WhatsApp-first contact.

### Brand Personality
Credible, strategic, and approachable. The voice is that of a calm independent
specialist: direct, practical, business-aware, and honest about what is proven.
It should never sound like a large anonymous agency or rely on hype.

### Aesthetic Direction
A warm editorial portfolio rather than a generic SaaS template: paper-toned
backgrounds, deep ink typography, a restrained terracotta accent, strong type
contrast, visible rules, and asymmetric compositions. The interface is light,
text-led, and tactile, with real project imagery used as evidence.

Avoid indigo-on-white template styling, glowing gradients, glass effects,
identical card grids, excessive rounded containers, generic technology imagery,
and decorative metrics without context.

### Design Principles
1. Lead with the buyer's desired outcome, then explain the specialist behind it.
2. Put verified proof before broad claims; never invent business results.
3. Maintain one clear homepage conversion path: apply to the free website program.
4. Make project work legible as problem, contribution, and concrete deliverable.
5. Use visual restraint, responsive layouts, and accessible interactions to
   demonstrate the same quality being sold.
