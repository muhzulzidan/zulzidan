# Campaign Strategy

## Objective

Generate qualified conversations that can become paid Starter Sprints, then use successful delivery to introduce the monthly Growth Team or Digital Department plans.

The commercial metric is paid Sprints—not impressions, video views, clicks, or unqualified chats.

## Ideal buyer

Primary roles:

- Founder or managing director
- Operations lead
- Marketing lead
- Executive director at a nonprofit or association

Organization profile:

- Small nonprofit, association, startup, or growing service business
- Has an existing website or digital operation
- Has a real backlog but lacks coordinated internal capacity
- Can approve a `$500 USD` pilot
- Is comfortable working remotely in English

Do not mix unrelated regions and audience narratives in the first test. Pick one market and one buyer context per ad set.

## Core problem

Digital work is fragmented across freelancers, agencies, and internal staff. Every task needs a new briefing, ownership is unclear, and the backlog grows faster than it ships.

## Offer

**Starter Sprint**

- `$500 USD`
- Five focused business days
- One clearly agreed outcome
- Short kickoff and scope confirmation
- One accountable point of contact
- Handoff and next-step recommendations

Good Sprint examples:

- A focused landing page
- Priority website fixes
- CMS cleanup
- Launch support
- A small, clearly bounded workflow improvement

Not promised:

- Unlimited requests
- A large application in five days
- Guaranteed revenue, rankings, leads, or business outcomes
- Round-the-clock staffing

## Message hierarchy

1. Pain: stop coordinating disconnected vendors.
2. Mechanism: one team, one priority queue, one accountable lead.
3. Entry offer: five focused days on one agreed outcome.
4. Proof: TASFRL case study, international delivery experience, and work since 2019.
5. CTA: message `STARTER` on WhatsApp.

## Brand voice

- Direct, calm, specific, and business-focused
- Show the work instead of using exaggerated adjectives
- Use `I lead the team` and `we deliver` consistently
- Explain boundaries clearly; do not imply exclusive full-time staffing

## Visual direction

Match the website:

- Forest: `#18201c`
- Cream: `#f4efe7`
- Terracotta: `#c74b2f`
- Peach: `#edc9ae`
- Editorial serif headlines with clean sans-serif labels
- Real founder, real work, real interfaces; avoid generic stock teams

## Primary call to action

`Message STARTER on WhatsApp to see if your priority fits.`
