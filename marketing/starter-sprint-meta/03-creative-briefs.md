# Creative Briefs

## Shared rules

- One advertisement communicates one problem and one promise.
- Put the hook in the first two seconds or first line.
- Show the founder, the work, or the workflow early.
- Burn in accurate subtitles; creative must still make sense without sound.
- Keep logos, headline, price, and CTA inside placement safe zones.
- End every conversion asset with `Message STARTER on WhatsApp`.
- Never display confidential client information or unsupported results.

## SS-V01 — Founder / one priority

Format: 20–25 second talking-head video  
Angle: accountable founder + simple entry offer

### Script

**0–3 seconds**  
“Still hiring a different freelancer for every website update, campaign, and automation task?”

**3–10 seconds**  
Show a task board, website screens, and brief working shots.  
“That fragmentation slows the work before it even begins.”

**10–20 seconds**  
“With the Zulzidan Starter Sprint, my team spends five focused business days on one clear digital priority—for $500 USD.”

**20–25 seconds**  
“Clear scope, one accountable lead, and a practical handoff. Message STARTER to see if your priority fits.”

On-screen headline: `One priority. Five focused days. $500.`

## SS-V02 — The backlog

Format: 15–20 second montage  
Angle: fragmented ownership vs. one priority queue

### Sequence

1. Hook: `Your backlog isn't the problem. Nobody owns it.`
2. Show scattered messages labeled website update, landing page, CMS fixes, and workflow.
3. Move the requests into one clean priority board.
4. Mark one realistic item complete.
5. Offer frame: `$500 / 5 days / 1 agreed outcome`.
6. CTA: `Stop coordinating. Start shipping. Message STARTER.`

## SS-V03 — TASFRL proof

Format: 25–30 second screen-led case study  
Angle: context compounds when one partner understands the organization

### Sequence

1. Hook: `What changes when one digital partner learns the whole organization?`
2. Show approved TASFRL website and system screens.
3. Describe only verified delivery: web, CMS, service journeys, content, workflows, and systems.
4. Explain that the relationship grew from digital support into ongoing collaboration.
5. Bridge: `You can begin with one focused five-day Sprint.`
6. CTA: `Message STARTER on WhatsApp.`

Required before approval: TASFRL usage permission and a claims check against the published case study.
