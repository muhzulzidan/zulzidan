## Canvas sizes

- Reels and Stories: `1080 × 1920` (`9:16`)
- Instagram/Facebook feed: `1080 × 1350` (`4:5`)
- Square fallback: `1080 × 1080` (`1:1`)
- Export images as high-quality JPG or PNG.
- Export video as MP4/H.264 with clear speech and no copyrighted audio.

## Shot list

- Founder looking directly into camera
- Founder working at a laptop
- Over-the-shoulder view of a non-confidential priority board
- Real planning call or team collaboration, with participant permission
- Close-ups of notes, wireframes, and task prioritization
- Mobile and desktop views of delivered projects
- Approved TASFRL screens
- A realistic completion/handoff moment

Do not fake a large office or anonymous team. If contributors cannot appear, frame the offer as a coordinated team led directly by Muhammad.

## Recording checklist

- Clean lens and quiet room
- Soft front light; avoid a bright window behind the speaker
- Phone at eye level; record 4K if available
- Leave space around the subject for 9:16 and 4:5 crops
- Record three hook takes and two complete takes
- Capture 5–8 seconds of every B-roll shot
- Record room tone
- Confirm names, browser tabs, messages, and dashboards contain no private information

## Editing checklist

- Hook is visible or spoken within two seconds
- Remove pauses before the hook
- Use burned-in subtitles and manually check spelling
- Use large, high-contrast text
- Keep one main thought on screen at a time
- Use real UI footage rather than decorative mockups when possible
- Show `$500 USD`, `5 business days`, and `1 agreed outcome` exactly
- End card remains readable long enough to act
- Review once with sound and once muted

## Naming convention

`SS-META-001_<asset-code>_<hook>_<ratio>_v<version>.<ext>`

Examples:

- `SS-META-001_SS-V01_different-freelancer_9x16_v01.mp4`
- `SS-META-001_SS-S01_offer-card_4x5_v03.png`

Never overwrite an approved export. Increase the version number.

## Review gates

1. **Message review:** accurate offer, audience, CTA, and no unsupported claim.
2. **Brand review:** colors, typography, tone, and founder/team representation.
3. **Technical review:** dimensions, audio, subtitles, safe zones, and export quality.
4. **Compliance review:** client permission, privacy, copyright, and destination behavior.
5. **Final approval:** status changed to `APPROVED` in the creative tracker.
