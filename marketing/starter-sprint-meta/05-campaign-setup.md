# Meta Campaign Setup

Interface labels can change. Confirm available options inside the account before publishing.

## Recommended first campaign

- Campaign name: `SS-META-001_PROSPECTING_WHATSAPP`
- Objective: Leads
- Conversion location: Messaging apps
- Destination: WhatsApp
- Performance goal: maximize conversations or the closest available qualified-conversation option
- Placements: Advantage+ placements, with placement-specific 9:16 and 4:5 assets
- CTA: Send WhatsApp Message

Avoid using Traffic as the primary objective when the commercial goal is a qualified conversation.

## Ad-set discipline

Begin with one market and one buyer context. Do not divide a small budget across many interests, countries, age bands, and ad sets.

Record these before launch:

- Market and timezone
- Language
- Buyer role/context
- Included and excluded locations
- Daily budget
- Start date and review date
- Response owner and operating hours

## WhatsApp welcome flow

### Greeting

Thanks for reaching out about the Zulzidan Starter Sprint. It is five focused business days on one clearly agreed digital priority for `$500 USD`. Please answer these short questions so I can confirm whether the Sprint fits.

### Qualification questions

1. What is your organization and website URL?
2. What single priority would you like to move forward?
3. Why is this important now?
4. Is there a fixed deadline or launch date?
5. If the scope fits, are you comfortable with the `$500 USD` Sprint budget?

### Next-step promise

Thanks. Muhammad will review this and reply with fit, likely scope, and the next step within the published response window.

Do not promise a response time until the responsible person can consistently meet it.

## Ad names

`<campaign>_<audience>_<asset-code>_<hook>_<version>`

Example:

`SS-META-001_AU-OPS_SS-V01_FREELANCER_v01`

## Budget test rule

Choose a test budget the business can afford to lose while learning. Give each concept enough delivery to collect signal, but do not scale based on cheap clicks alone. The weekly scorecard must show qualified conversations and paid Sprints before budget increases.

## Pre-publish QA

- Correct Facebook Page and Instagram account
- Correct WhatsApp number
- Correct market, language, and schedule
- Offer says USD everywhere
- Website or messaging destination opens correctly
- UTMs survive the click where a URL is used
- 9:16 and 4:5 placement assets are assigned
- Auto-enhancements are reviewed; disable any that distort brand, text, or screenshots
- Comments and inbox have an owner
- Every ad is entered in the test log
