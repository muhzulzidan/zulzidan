# Content and Launch Plan

## Phase 1 — trust foundation

Publish these as organic posts before paid launch:

1. `SS-V01` — founder explains the fragmented-vendor problem and Starter Sprint.
2. `SS-V03` — TASFRL mini case study.
3. `SS-C01` — Starter Sprint explainer carousel.

Pin the three posts where the platform supports it. Complete the bio, website link, profile image, and contact method so profile visitors see a coherent offer.

## Phase 2 — paid validation

Launch three genuinely different concepts:

- Founder/accountability: `SS-V01`
- Pain/backlog: `SS-V02`
- Direct offer: `SS-S01`

Do not launch minor color or caption variations as if they were different concepts. Keep audience and offer stable while testing the creative angle.

## Phase 3 — proof and objection testing

After the first review:

- Add `SS-V03` if proof appears to be the missing ingredient.
- Add `SS-V04` if timezone, location, or trust objections appear repeatedly.
- Add `SS-S02` for a simpler founder-led feed treatment.

## Weekly review agenda

1. Are messages coming from the intended buyer?
2. Do prospects understand `$500`, five days, and one outcome?
3. Which objection appears most often?
4. Which creative produced qualified—not merely cheap—conversations?
5. Can delivery and response capacity support more spend?
6. What single variable will the next test change?
