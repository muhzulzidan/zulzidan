## Processing record

All derivatives:

- Preserve the original identity, lighting, setting, and expression.
- Use a deterministic 4:5 crop.
- Are exported at `1600 × 2000` WebP, quality 82.
- Contain no generative alteration.
- Keep original files untouched.

## Usage guidance

- Use the calm portrait when accountability and competence are the main message.
- Use the smiling portrait when approachability and direct contact are the main message.
- Use the collaboration photograph only as secondary context; it does not independently prove digital delivery.
- Confirm the other visible participant has approved public advertising use before publishing the collaboration photograph.
- Do not use the older formal portrait beside the newer red-background portraits in the same journey; the appearance and tone are inconsistent.

## Source handling

The high-resolution originals are currently under `public/zulzidan/`. They should eventually move to the external raw-media archive described in `assets/README.md`; only the optimized derivatives need to remain publicly served by the website.
